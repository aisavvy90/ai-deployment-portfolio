## The adoption problem

Enterprise AI adoption does not fail only because of technology. It also fails when learning is too generic, governance is unclear or people cannot connect a tool to the work they do every day.

## The approach

I designed practical enablement around three layers:

1. **Understand** — establish useful mental models, limits and responsible-use boundaries.
2. **Apply** — demonstrate real operating use cases rather than abstract prompts.
3. **Adopt** — reinforce usage through live practice, feedback, templates and operating guidance.

## Public outcome

**Enabled hundreds of practitioners** through live AI demonstrations, applied learning and adoption support.
