# Disclosure and Confidentiality

This repository is a public, sanitised portfolio of AI deployment work.

## Included

- public-safe problem statements;
- aggregated outcomes approved for external use;
- generic architecture and operating patterns;
- my contribution across discovery, design, building, deployment and adoption;
- product screenshots that contain no client or proprietary information.

## Excluded

- client identities;
- internal product names;
- exact commercial scope or mandate values;
- proprietary source code and business rules;
- credentials, environment configuration and platform identifiers;
- internal emails, stakeholder names and confidential screenshots.

Production repositories for enterprise systems remain private by design. Architecture, testing methodology, trade-offs and failure modes can be discussed in an appropriate interview setting.
